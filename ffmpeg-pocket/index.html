    About large files and offline use
    
Video processing is heavy. Short clips and ordinary phone videos are the safest. Very large files can exceed iPhone browser memory. Keep Chrome open while a job runs.

    
After the first successful load, Chrome may keep the media engine cached. You can also use Chrome → Share → Add to Home Screen for a one-tap shortcut.

  

  
Processing happens locally in the browser. The page does not send your selected media to a server.
